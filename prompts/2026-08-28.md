A consistently applied custom modern geometric sans-serif typeface, ensuring robust legibility and brand consistency across all elements.

*   **Secondary Headings (H3, H4, H5, H6):** A versatile, highly legible sans-serif that seamlessly complements the primary heading font, ensuring a clear and intuitive information hierarchy.
*   **Body Text:** A clean, accessible sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Source Sans Pro) meticulously optimized for screen readability, featuring generous line height for comfortable, extended reading.
*   **Accent/Call-to-Action Text:** The primary heading font, typically in a slightly bolder weight or utilizing the accent color for prominent emphasis.

# Grid System
A robust and flexible 12-column Swiss grid system, built on an 8px base unit.
*   **Columns:** 12 columns, ensuring exceptional alignment, modularity, and scalability across resolutions.
*   **Gutters:** Consistent 24px (3x 8px) gutters between columns, providing clear visual separation.
*   **Margins:** Generous page margins (e.g., 80-120px on desktop) to elegantly frame content and amplify the sense of expansive white space.
*   All design elements will precisely snap to this grid, creating an impeccably organized and architecturally precise visual language.

# Spacing System
A comprehensive 8pt spacing system will be universally applied for all vertical and horizontal spacing, padding, and margins, ensuring absolute visual harmony and consistency.
*   **Base Unit:** 8px
*   **Scale:** Multiples of 8 (e.g., 8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px, 80px, 96px, 128px, etc.)
*   This system guarantees a meticulously consistent design, contributing to a sense of precise craftsmanship and professional integrity throughout the layout.

# Components

    *   **Primary:** Solid fill with the accent color (Solar Yellow/Orange), crisp geometric shape, clean white sans-serif text. Minimal border-radius (e.g., 4px). Subtle hover effect (e.g., slight color darkening or subtle fill change, no "lift" shadow).
    *   **Secondary:** Outline style with the Deep Blue or Dark Gray border, transparent background, text in matching color. Subtle hover (fill with primary blue or darken border).
    *   **Text/Ghost:** Just text, minimal styling, for tertiary actions.
    *   All buttons will have generous padding (e.g., 16px vertical, 32px horizontal) derived from the 8pt grid.

*   **Cards:**
    *   Clean, minimalist design. Defined by a crisp 1px border in a light gray or a subtle background color difference, not shadows.
    *   Geometric shapes, minimal border-radius (e.g., 4px).
    *   Clear title, concise text, and optional geometric icon or image placeholder.
    *   Generous internal padding.
    *   No excessive glassmorphism or heavy shadows.

*   **Forms:**
    *   Input fields with a clean 1px border (light gray), white background, and clear text input.

    *   Consistent stroke weight and corner radius.
    *   Reflecting themes of electronics, energy, connectivity, and modern business.

    *   Colors derived from the main palette (Deep Blue, Dark Gray, Solar Yellow/Orange).

*   **Dividers:**
    *   Minimal, subtle 1px horizontal lines in light gray (e.g., #E0E0E0) to gracefully separate sections or content blocks without disrupting the white space.

# Interaction & Animation
Subtle, refined, and purposeful interactions that profoundly enhance user experience without being distracting or frivolous.
*   **Hover Effects:**
    *   **Buttons:** A very slight background color darkening or a subtle outline change. No aggressive "lift" or shadow effects.
    *   **Navigation Links:** A precise underline appears or text color subtly shifts to the accent color.
    *   **Cards/Interactive Elements:** A very subtle 1px border highlight or a slight background tint change.
*   **Transitions:** Smooth, fast (e.g., 200-300ms) transitions for navigation, modal displays, and content loading. Linear or ease-out timing functions for a crisp, responsive feel.
*   **Micro-interactions:**
    *   Form field focus states (a precise border highlight).
    *   Loaders: Minimalistic, geometric spinning icon or a sleek progress bar.
    *   Confirmation messages: Discreet, elegantly designed, well-placed toasts.
    *   All animations should feel premium, responsive, and contribute to the "pixel-perfect" impression of the interface.

# Mobile Responsive Design
The desktop layout will gracefully and intelligently adapt to mobile devices (smartphones and tablets) while meticulously maintaining the premium, minimalist aesthetic and critical information hierarchy.
*   **Content Prioritization:** Essential information will be supremely prominent, with secondary details thoughtfully accessible via accordions or tabbed interfaces when necessary.
*   **Navigation:** A clean hamburger menu (icon-based, geometric) with an elegant overlay or slide-out navigation for full menu access.
*   **Layout Adaptation:**
    *   Multi-column layouts will collapse intelligently into a single-column stack on smaller screens.
    *   Imagery will be optimized for mobile (responsive images, lazy loading) and scale appropriately without distortion.
    *   Typography will fluidly adjust in size to ensure optimal readability, while strictly maintaining the established hierarchy.
    *   Buttons and interactive elements will be meticulously designed to be touch-friendly with larger, easily tappable targets.
*   **Spacing:** Margins and padding will be precisely adjusted to optimize for smaller screens while rigorously preserving key negative space principles.
*   **Performance:** The mobile experience will be exceptionally fast and fluid, with optimized assets and minimal, purposeful animations.
