# Company Name
Nexus Electronics

# Industry
Electronics, Electrical Accessories, Solar & Renewable Energy

# Website Purpose
To establish Nexus Electronics as a premium, innovative, and reliable leader in the electronics and renewable energy sectors. The website will serve to showcase a curated range of high-quality products, educate the target audience on sustainable solutions, build brand trust, generate inquiries, and facilitate direct sales or lead generation for homeowners, businesses, contractors, and industrial clients. It aims to position the brand as intelligent, future-ready, and committed to sustainability.

# Target Audience
*   **Homeowners:** Seeking reliable, modern electrical accessories and sustainable home energy solutions.
*   **Businesses:** Looking for efficient, scalable electrical and renewable energy systems for commercial properties.
*   **Contractors:** Requiring high-quality, dependable components for their projects, seeking a trusted supplier.
*   **Industrial & Commercial Customers:** Demanding robust, high-performance electronics and solar solutions for large-scale applications.

# Brand Personality
Innovative, reliable, trusted, intelligent, sustainable, future-ready, premium, professional, minimalist, modern, geometric, timeless, precise.

# Design Direction
The design direction is strictly architectural, modern, and premium, embodying an enterprise-grade aesthetic. It will be typography-driven, adhering to a meticulous Swiss Grid and an editorial layout approach. The interface will feature abundant large white space, creating a sense of calm and clarity, contributing to a timeless design. The UI will be professional, pixel-perfect, and fully responsive. The visual language will be minimal, ensuring a clean information hierarchy and an uncluttered user experience.

# Visual Identity
Clean, geometric, and minimalist, deeply integrated with the brand's abstract "N" logo symbol. The visual identity will communicate precision, quality, and innovation, with a focus on clean lines, crisp edges, and a sophisticated, understated elegance. The overall impression should be one of a highly intelligent and trustworthy entity.

# Color Palette
*   **Primary Accent:** Deep Blue (e.g., #0A2E6D) – Represents reliability, intelligence, and professionalism.
*   **Secondary Accent:** Solar Yellow/Orange (e.g., #FFB800) – Symbolizes innovation, energy, and sustainability (solar).
*   **Neutral Dark:** Dark Gray (e.g., #333333) – For secondary text, subtle backgrounds, and UI elements, providing strong contrast.
*   **Neutral Light:** Pure White (e.g., #FFFFFF) – Dominant background and negative space, emphasizing clarity and premium feel.
*   **Text/High Contrast:** Black (e.g., #000000) – For primary headlines and critical text elements, ensuring maximum legibility.

# Typography
*   **Primary Font:** A custom modern geometric sans-serif (or a carefully selected equivalent like Montserrat, Gotham, or Proxima Nova, but leaning towards a custom or less common choice to enhance premium feel). Used for all headings, primary navigation, and prominent display text. Emphasize precise kerning and clear hierarchy.
*   **Body Font:** A highly legible, clean sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Open Sans, Source Sans Pro, or a secondary weight of the primary font family). Used for body text, paragraphs, and functional UI elements, ensuring readability at all sizes.
*   **Emphasis:** Strategic use of varying weights (light, regular, medium, bold) and sizes to create a strong visual hierarchy without introducing multiple font families.

# Layout Structure

*   **Header / Navigation:**
    *   **Structure:** Minimalist, sticky, and highly intuitive. Logo (Nexus Electronics) precisely aligned to the left. Main navigation to the right, featuring key sections like "Products," "Solutions," "About Us," "Sustainability," and "Contact."
    *   **Elements:** Subtle utility navigation for search, account, or language selection. Clean, geometric iconography.
    *   **Behavior:** Shrinks slightly on scroll to maintain prominence while optimizing screen real estate.
*   **Hero Section:**
    *   **Content:** Large, impactful headline (primary message), concise sub-headline (value proposition), and a clear, singular Call-to-Action.
    *   **Visuals:** Premium, abstract conceptual render depicting energy flow, circuit connections, or a sophisticated product detail, rather than a generic photo. Ample white space around the core content.
    *   **Typography:** Dominant, large-scale typography, setting the tone for the entire site.
*   **Feature Sections:**
    *   **Purpose:** To highlight key product categories, core services, or brand pillars (e.g., innovation, sustainability, reliability).
    *   **Structure:** Utilizes a strict Swiss Grid system. Each feature presented in a clean card or module format with minimal borders, strong visual elements (custom icons or abstract imagery), a concise heading, and brief descriptive text.
    *   **Layout:** Typically 2, 3, or 4 columns, adaptable to screen size.
*   **Content Sections:**
    *   **Purpose:** For in-depth information, company story, sustainability initiatives, or detailed product explanations.
    *   **Structure:** Editorial layout, prioritizing readability with generous line height and paragraph spacing. Large white space around text blocks.
    *   **Elements:** Clear headings, subheadings, bullet points, and high-quality, relevant abstract visuals or product photography integrated seamlessly.
*   **Call-to-Action (CTA):**
    *   **Placement:** Strategically integrated within relevant sections, as well as standalone prompt areas.
    *   **Design:** Consistent button style (primary/secondary). Clear, concise, and action-oriented messaging (e.g., "Explore Products," "Learn More," "Get a Quote").
*   **Footer:**
    *   **Content:** Essential navigation links (categorized), contact information, physical address (if applicable), social media links (using custom, minimalist icons), privacy policy, terms of service, and copyright information.
    *   **Design:** Clean, minimalist, and compact, maintaining the overall brand aesthetic. Subtle typography.

# Grid System
A robust 12-column Swiss Grid system, providing precise alignment and a modular structure for all content elements. All components will snap to this grid, ensuring pixel-perfect horizontal and vertical rhythm. The grid will be flexible enough to accommodate various content types while maintaining a consistent visual framework.

# Spacing System
A rigorous 8-point spacing system (e.g., 8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px, etc.) will govern all internal and external spacing, padding, and margins. This ensures consistency and contributes to the clean, architectural feel and generous white space.

# Components

*   **Buttons:**
    *   **Primary:** Deep Blue solid fill, white text. Sharp, geometric corners (minimal to no border-radius). Subtle color shift on hover.
    *   **Secondary:** Dark Gray outline, Deep Blue text. Transparent or white fill. Subtle outline weight change or background fill on hover.
    *   **Tertiary:** Text link style, Deep Blue text, subtle underline or color change on hover.
    *   **Design:** All buttons will be minimalist, with clear typography and consistent sizing, reflecting the geometric aesthetic.
*   **Cards:**
    *   **Design:** Clean, minimalist, geometric. Ample internal padding. Defined by a subtle thin line border (Dark Gray or Deep Blue) or simply by the surrounding white space. No shadows to maintain a flat design aesthetic.
    *   **Content:** Consistent structure for content display (e.g., image/icon top, title, description, CTA).
*   **Forms:**
    *   **Inputs:** Clean, minimalist text fields with subtle thin borders (Dark Gray). Clear labels placed above input fields. Focus state with a Deep Blue border.
    *   **Layout:** Structured for optimal readability and data entry, with clear error messages and confirmation states.
*   **Icons:**
    *   **Style:** Custom, geometric, line-based or solid vector icons. Must perfectly align with the brand's minimalist and precise aesthetic. No stock icons.
    *   **Usage:** For navigation, features, social media, and functional elements.
*   **Dividers:**
    *   **Style:** Minimal, thin, horizontal lines (Dark Gray or a very light shade of gray) or simply generous white space to separate content sections. Avoid heavy or decorative dividers.

# Imagery Style
The imagery will be highly curated and premium, emphasizing professionalism and conceptual clarity.
*   **Abstract Visuals:** Focus on sophisticated, abstract 3D renders or conceptual digital art depicting circuit connections, energy flow, geometric patterns, and clean lines relevant to electronics and solar energy. These should be high-resolution, vector-like in precision, and integrated seamlessly into the layout.
*   **Photography:** Studio-quality, high-resolution product photography with clean backgrounds (white or dark gray), showcasing the design and precision of Nexus Electronics products. Lifestyle photography should be minimal, highly curated, and focus on the professional, sustainable use of products rather than generic stock imagery.
*   **Restriction:** Absolutely no stock AI imagery, no cartoon illustrations, no busy backgrounds. The imagery must feel intentional, custom, and premium.

# Interaction & Animation
All interactions and animations will be subtle, purposeful, and enhance the user experience without being distracting or frivolous.
*   **Hover Effects:** Minimal and elegant. Buttons may exhibit a subtle color shift or a very slight upward lift. Links will have a discreet underline appearance or a slight text color change.
*   **Transitions:** Fast and smooth, primarily using subtle fades or slides for section changes, modal openings, and content loading.
*   **Micro-interactions:** Small, delightful details such as subtle icon transformations on hover, clean form input focus states, and discreet feedback for user actions (e.g., success messages, loading indicators). No excessive or jarring animations.

# Mobile Responsive Design
The desktop layout will gracefully adapt to mobile devices, prioritizing content and maintaining the core design principles.
*   **Layout Adaptation:** The multi-column Swiss Grid will collapse into a single-column stack on smaller screens. Features and content sections will re-order logically.
*   **Navigation:** The primary navigation will transform into a clean, minimalist hamburger menu, revealing an off-canvas or full-screen navigation overlay.
*   **Typography:** Font sizes and line heights will be optimized for readability on smaller screens, while maintaining the established typographic hierarchy.
*   **Spacing:** Spacing will be adjusted to ensure optimal readability and tap targets, while still preserving sufficient white space.
*   **Images:** Images will be optimized for mobile performance and responsive scaling.
*   **Touch Targets:** All interactive elements will have appropriately large touch targets for ease of use on touch screens.

    *   `--fs-button: 1rem;`
    *   `--line-height-heading: 1.1;`
    *   `--line-height-body: 1.6;`
*   **Color Tokens:**
    *   `--color-primary: #0A2E6D;` (Deep Blue)
    *   `--color-accent: #FFB800;` (Solar Yellow/Orange)
    *   `--color-text-dark: #333333;` (Dark Gray)
    *   `--color-text-light: #FFFFFF;` (White)
    *   `--color-background-primary: #FFFFFF;` (White)
    *   `--color-background-secondary: #F8F8F8;` (Very Light Gray for subtle contrast sections)
    *   `--color-border-subtle: #E0E0E0;` (Light Gray for minimalist borders)
*   **Spacing Scale (using `px` for pixel-perfect base):**
    *   `--spacing-xs: 8px;`
    *   `--spacing-sm: 16px;`
    *   `--spacing-md: 24px;`
    *   `--spacing-lg: 32px;`
    *   `--spacing-xl: 48px;`
    *   `--spacing-xxl: 64px;`
    *   `--spacing-xxxl: 96px;`
    *   `--spacing-xxxxl: 128px;`
*   **Border Radius:**
    *   `--radius-none: 0px;` (Predominant for geometric aesthetic)
    *   `--radius-sm: 2px;` (Minimal rounding for subtle details, if absolutely necessary, but generally avoid)
*   **Shadows:**
    *   `--shadow-none: none;` (Strictly adhering to flat design principles; no shadows for elements)
*   **Component Variants:**
    *   **Buttons:** `primary` (default), `secondary` (outlined), `tertiary` (text link), `disabled`, `hover`, `active`, `loading`.
    *   **Inputs:** `default`, `focused`, `error`, `disabled`.
    *   **Cards:** `default`, `hover` (subtle border highlight).
    *   **Icons:** `default` (line), `filled` (if applicable for specific states).
