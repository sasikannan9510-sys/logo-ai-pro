# Company Name
Nexus Electronics

# Industry
Electronics, Electrical Accessories, Solar & Renewable Energy

# Website Purpose
To establish Nexus Electronics as a leading, trusted, and innovative provider of premium electrical and renewable energy solutions. To educate, engage, and convert a diverse audience (homeowners, businesses, contractors, industrial/commercial customers) by showcasing product excellence, sustainability initiatives, and expert services, ultimately driving inquiries, consultations, and sales. The website should reinforce brand authority, communicate core values (innovation, reliability, sustainability), and present a seamless user experience.

# Target Audience
*   **Homeowners:** Seeking reliable, efficient, and sustainable home electrical solutions and renewable energy systems.
*   **Businesses:** Requiring robust and scalable electrical accessories and energy solutions for their operations.
*   **Contractors:** Looking for high-quality, dependable components and systems for installation projects.
*   **Industrial & Commercial Customers:** Needing advanced, durable, and efficient electronics and renewable energy infrastructure.

# Brand Personality
Innovative, reliable, trusted, intelligent, sustainable, future-ready, premium, professional, precise.

# Design Direction
A premium, minimalist, and modern aesthetic with strong influences from Swiss graphic design principles. The design should feel architectural, timeless, and sophisticated, emphasizing clean lines, geometric precision, and an exceptional focus on typography. It should convey innovation and trustworthiness through a highly structured, professional, and visually uncluttered interface.

# Visual Identity
Clean, crisp, and sophisticated. The visual identity should be heavily typography-driven, using a geometric sans-serif that reflects precision and modernity. Imagery should be high-quality, authentic, and purposeful. The overall style should be flat with carefully considered use of brand colors, prioritizing ample white space to create a sense of calm, clarity, and premium elegance. The brand's abstract "N" logo with circuit connections and power flow elements should be subtly echoed in geometric patterns or clean line work throughout the UI without becoming busy.

# Color Palette
*   **Primary Accent (Reliability/Professionalism):** Deep Blue (e.g., `#0A2E5B` - a rich, dark, authoritative blue)
*   **Secondary Accent (Energy/Innovation/Sustainability):** Solar Yellow/Orange (e.g., `#FFA500` - a vibrant, warm, yet sophisticated orange, evocative of solar energy)
*   **Primary Text/Dark Elements:** Dark Gray (e.g., `#333333` - for readability and contrast)
*   **Secondary Text/Strong Contrast:** Black (e.g., `#000000`)
*   **Background/Clean Canvas:** Pure White (e.g., `#FFFFFF`)
*   **Subtle Background/Borders:** Light Gray (e.g., `#F0F0F0` or `#EAEAEA` - for subtle visual separation)

# Typography
*   **Headings (H1-H6):** A custom modern geometric sans-serif font (e.g., a variant of Montserrat, Avenir, Gotham, or a bespoke font like "Nexus Sans"). Emphasize precise kerning, clear hierarchy through weight and size variations, maintaining a clean and commanding presence.
*   **Body Text:** A highly legible, clean, and modern sans-serif font (e.g., Inter, Open Sans, Lato, or Rubik) that complements the heading font, ensuring optimal readability across all devices.
*   **Emphasis:** Use font weight (bold/semi-bold), color (dark gray/black), and slight letter spacing for emphasis, maintaining consistency.

# Layout Structure

*   **Header / Navigation:**
    *   Minimalist, fixed-position header with the Nexus Electronics logo (abstract 'N') on the left.
    *   Primary navigation links (e.g., Products, Solutions, About Us, Resources, Contact) centrally aligned or right-aligned.
    *   Clean, subtle hover states.
    *   Secondary actions like a "Get a Quote" or "Shop Now" button on the far right, using the Solar Yellow/Orange accent color.
    *   Subtle divider or shadow to indicate separation from the hero section.

*   **Hero Section:**
    *   Full-width, high-impact visual (professional, architectural photography or a subtle, abstract motion graphic related to energy/circuits).
    *   Large, bold, typography-driven headline conveying innovation or key value proposition (e.g., "Powering Tomorrow, Today").
    *   Concise sub-headline.
    *   Prominent primary Call-to-Action (CTA) button, using the Solar Yellow/Orange accent color.
    *   Optional: Subtle scrolling indicator or animated element.

*   **Feature Sections:**
    *   Grid-based layouts (2, 3, or 4 columns) showcasing key product categories (e.g., Solar Solutions, Electrical Accessories, Industrial Electronics).
    *   Each feature block/card should be clean, with a high-quality product image or icon, a bold title, and a brief description.
    *   "Learn More" or "Explore" CTA on each card.
    *   Ample white space surrounding each feature.

*   **Content Sections:**
    *   Editorial-style blocks for "Our Story," "Sustainability Commitment," "Why Choose Nexus," or "Case Studies."
    *   Asymmetrical layouts with large, impactful typography, generous line heights, and strategically placed, high-quality imagery.
    *   Clear, concise paragraphs broken up for readability.
    *   May include testimonials or statistics presented in a clean, infographic-like manner.

*   **Call-to-Action (CTA) Sections:**
    *   Dedicated full-width or prominent blocks encouraging specific actions (e.g., "Request a Consultation," "View All Products," "Become a Partner").
    *   Large, distinct primary and secondary CTA buttons.
    *   May feature a background image or a solid brand color block with white text for maximum contrast.

*   **Footer:**
    *   Multi-column structure, clean and organized.
    *   Left column: Nexus Electronics logo, brief brand statement.
    *   Middle columns: Navigation links (Products, Solutions, Company, Resources, Support) grouped logically.
    *   Right column: Contact information, minimalist social media icons.
    *   Bottom strip: Copyright information, privacy policy, terms of service.
    *   All text should be legible, using appropriate hierarchy.

# Grid System
Strict 12-column Swiss-style grid system. Emphasis on strong vertical rhythm, precise horizontal alignment, and consistent baselines. Content should snap to the grid, creating a sense of order, structure, and professionalism. Columns may be combined for large content blocks, always respecting the underlying grid.

# Spacing System
A highly intentional and generous spacing system, primarily based on an 8px or 16px baseline grid. Large white space is paramount, acting as a design element itself to enhance clarity, breathability, and premium feel. Consistent spacing should be applied between sections, content blocks, and within components to create visual harmony and a clear information hierarchy.

# Components

*   **Buttons:**
    *   Minimalist, geometric, rectangular with a very subtle border-radius (2-4px).
    *   **Primary:** Deep Blue fill, white text.
    *   **Secondary:** White fill, Deep Blue border, Deep Blue text.
    *   **Accent:** Solar Yellow/Orange fill, Dark Gray or Black text for high contrast.
    *   Clean, subtle hover effects (e.g., slight color shift, subtle increase in opacity, or a very faint box-shadow for elevation).

*   **Cards:**
    *   Clean, modular design with crisp, 1px light gray borders or very subtle, almost imperceptible shadows for elevation.
    *   White or light gray backgrounds.
    *   Clear visual hierarchy for titles, images, and descriptions.
    *   Ample internal padding.

*   **Forms:**
    *   Clean, minimalist input fields with subtle borders.
    *   Clear labels above or beside input fields.
    *   Focus state indicated by a thin Solar Yellow/Orange border or subtle glow.
    *   Simple, geometric checkboxes/radio buttons.
    *   Error messages that are clear and concise.

*   **Icons:**
    *   Custom-designed, geometric, line-art or solid icons.
    *   Consistent style with the abstract "N" logo, reinforcing circuit connections, power flow, and clean lines.
    *   Used sparingly and meaningfully to support content, not clutter.

*   **Dividers:**
    *   Minimal, thin horizontal lines (e.g., 1px light gray) used strategically to separate sections or content within a component, or replaced by generous white space.

# Imagery Style
High-resolution, professional, and authentic photography is preferred.
*   **Product Photography:** Clean, studio-quality shots of Nexus Electronics products against stark white or minimalist architectural backgrounds, emphasizing precision and design.
*   **Lifestyle/Application Photography:** Architectural photography showcasing solar panels integrated into modern, clean buildings; well-lit interiors with visible smart electronics; industrial settings depicting advanced machinery with Nexus components. Focus on composition, light, and a modern, aspirational aesthetic.
*   **Abstract Visuals:** When photography isn't suitable, subtle, abstract geometric patterns or clean line graphics (like circuit board traces or energy flow diagrams) can be used as background textures or decorative elements, always maintaining a premium, non-busy feel. Avoid stock AI imagery, illustrations, or overly artistic/distracting visuals.

# Interaction & Animation
All interactions should be subtle, refined, and responsive, enhancing the premium feel without being distracting.
*   **Hover Effects:** Gentle color shifts or opacity changes on buttons and links; subtle card lifts with a very faint shadow; minimalist underline animations on navigation items.
*   **Transitions:** Smooth, elegant fades or slides for content reveals on scroll, section transitions, and modal openings.
*   **Micro-interactions:** Input field focus state animations; subtle loading indicators; clear, non-intrusive feedback for form submissions (success/error states); subtle parallax scrolling on hero images.

# Mobile Responsive Design
The desktop layout should fluidly adapt to smaller screen sizes while maintaining the core design principles:
*   **Grid Adaptation:** The 12-column grid will collapse to a simpler 1, 2, or 3-column layout, stacking content vertically.
*   **Navigation:** Primary navigation will transform into a minimalist hamburger menu, revealing a full-screen or side-drawer navigation on tap.
*   **Content Prioritization:** Essential information and CTAs will be prioritized and presented clearly.
*   **Typography:** Font sizes will be adjusted for optimal legibility on smaller screens, maintaining hierarchy.
*   **Imagery:** Images will scale appropriately, ensuring fast loading and visual clarity without loss of quality.
*   **Touch Targets:** Buttons and interactive elements will be adequately sized for finger touch.
*   **Spacing:** White space will be maintained generously where possible to prevent a cluttered look, even on mobile.

# Design System

*   **Typography Scale (based on a base of 16px):**
    *   `--font-size-h1: 4.0rem;` (64px)
    *   `--font-size-h2: 3.0rem;` (48px)
    *   `--font-size-h3: 2.25rem;` (36px)
    *   `--font-size-h4: 1.5rem;` (24px)
    *   `--font-size-body-lg: 1.125rem;` (18px)
    *   `--font-size-body-md: 1.0rem;` (16px)
    *   `--font-size-body-sm: 0.875rem;` (14px)
    *   `--font-size-caption: 0.75rem;` (12px)

*   **Color Tokens:**
    *   `--color-primary-deep-blue: #0A2E5B;`
    *   `--color-accent-solar-orange: #FFA500;`
    *   `--color-text-dark-gray: #333333;`
    *   `--color-text-black: #000000;`
    *   `--color-background-white: #FFFFFF;`
    *   `--color-background-light-gray: #F0F0F0;`
    *   `--color-border-light-gray: #EAEAEA;`

*   **Spacing Scale (based on 8px grid):**
    *   `--spacing-xxs: 4px;`
    *   `--spacing-xs: 8px;`
    *   `--spacing-sm: 16px;`
    *   `--spacing-md: 24px;`
    *   `--spacing-lg: 32px;`
    *   `--spacing-xl: 48px;`
    *   `--spacing-xxl: 64px;`
    *   `--spacing-xxxl: 96px;`
    *   `--spacing-xxxxl: 128px;`

*   **Border Radius:**
    *   `--border-radius-none: 0px;`
    *   `--border-radius-sm: 2px;` (for minimal softness on buttons/cards)
    *   `--border-radius-md: 4px;`

*   **Shadows (Subtle, for elevation only):**
    *   `--shadow-none: none;`
    *   `--shadow-sm: 0px 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04);` (for subtle card elevation on hover)
    *   `--shadow-md: 0px 4px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08);` (for modals/dropdowns)

*   **Component Variants:**
    *   **Button:** Primary, Secondary, Accent, Ghost (transparent background, colored border/text).
    *   **Card:** Default (subtle border), Elevated (on hover, uses `--shadow-sm`).
    *   **Input Field:** Default, Focused, Error, Disabled.
    *   **Icon:** Line (default), Solid (hover/active state).
